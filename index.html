LifeDrop Privacy Policy

LifeDrop ("the app", "we", "us") is a free blood donation app for Pakistan. It helps people who need blood to find and contact volunteer blood donors. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Who we are

Developer: Hafiz Ali Ahmed
Contact email: myxdirty@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (the password is handled securely by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it).
  • Donor profile (only if you register as a donor): name, phone number, blood group, province, district, tehsil, area, availability, and last donation date.
  • Blood requests (only if you post one): patient name, blood group, number of bags, urgency, hospital, location, phone number, and a note.
  • Hospitals you add: hospital name and location, which are reviewed before they are shown to others.
  • Reports: if you report a donor, request, or hospital, we store the reason, your user ID, and the reported item so we can review it.

We do not collect your GPS or device location. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To let people search for donors by blood group and location.
  • To show blood requests to other signed-in users.
  • To review reports and keep the app safe from fake or abusive content.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your phone number is not displayed on screen. Signed-in users can contact a donor or requester only through the Call and WhatsApp buttons.
  • Donor profile details (name, blood group, location, availability) are visible to other signed-in users of the app.
  • Because the data is stored in our database, signed-in users' devices receive the phone number in order to place a call or WhatsApp message. Please register as a donor only if you are comfortable being contacted this way.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign users in and to store data. Google acts as a data processor on our behalf. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Blood requests expire automatically after 7 days.
  • Your account and donor profile stay until you delete them.
  • Reports and approved hospital names may be kept after account deletion because they protect other users and are not linked to your personal profile.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Home, Settings, Delete my account. This deletes your login and your donor profile.

You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing myxdirty@gmail.com from your registered email. We will delete it within 30 days.

More details: Account deletion page.

8. Children

LifeDrop is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

9. Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase using access rules so that only signed-in users can read it and only you can change your own profile. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Medical disclaimer

LifeDrop only connects people. We do not check donors' health or blood group. Always confirm blood group and safety at a hospital or licensed blood bank before donating or receiving blood.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.

12. Contact us

Questions? Email myxdirty@gmail.com.
