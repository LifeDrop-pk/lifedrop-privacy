LifeDrop: Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your LifeDrop account and your data. The app is developed by Hafiz Ali Ahmed.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the LifeDrop app and sign in.
  2. On the Home screen, tap the Settings icon.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, send an email to myxdirty@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my LifeDrop account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password).
  • Your donor profile (name, phone number, blood group, location, availability, last donation date).
  • Your blood requests.

What may be kept

  • Reports submitted about users, requests, or hospitals, because they help keep other users safe. They are not linked to a public profile.
  • Names of approved hospitals you added, because they are public information that helps other users.

Blood requests also expire automatically after 7 days.
